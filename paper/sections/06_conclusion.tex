\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
We described a language-coordinated robot team whose roles are separated by interface, so that a failure has an owner, and two training signals that follow from the separation: counterfactual Eye credit and team hindsight relabeling with a replay check.
One success definition scores a symbolic world, a pose-level simulator and Isaac Lab, so plan validity and physical success can be compared.
An audit of the upstream level-2 scorer shows, on a synthetic case, how random start positions can make scores depend on luck.
The system is implemented and its mechanisms are checked on a fixture; the evaluation protocol is fixed and every trained-model, benchmark and physics result is pending.
The next step is to fill \cref{tab:trackA,tab:main,tab:ablations,tab:credit,tab:attribution}, and to report them under their evidence classes.
